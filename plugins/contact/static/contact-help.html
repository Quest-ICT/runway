Contact — help & reference

The Contact plugin adds a contact form to your site and stores each submission (and can email it), which you read here in the console. The form is a content widget: it lives on a normal, editable site page. You can add it to any page yourself, or install a ready-made contact page in one click from the settings.

Install a ready-made contact page

On the Contact settings page, the Contact page card installs a ready-made page carrying the form (at /contact). It then becomes an ordinary site page — it appears under Pages, and you can edit its template, restyle it, move it or give it a different URL. Installing is idempotent: it never overwrites an existing page. It also follows your site's publishing rules: on a site that requires review it waits in the review queue (unless you may approve pages), and when the site's quality checks block publishing it is kept as a draft and the answer lists what to fix. The answer always says which happened.

Or put the form on any page

<div id="contact" data-widget="contact/form"></div>

The id and data-widget must be on the same element. On submit the form posts to /contact/submit, which stores (and emails) the message and sends the visitor back to the page the form lives on, where they see your thank-you message. If the message was stored but your mail server could not be reached, the thank-you adds a short note that email delivery is delayed.

Read submissions

Every submission is stored inside your site's own folder and listed on the Contact settings page — newest first, with the name, email, message and time — and, when an SMTP relay is set, also emailed to you. A full backup of the site keeps the submissions. A transfer copy (the download meant for someone outside your organisation) leaves them out, because they are your visitors' personal data; its manifest lists what was left out.

Settings

  • Enable contact form — turn the form (and its submit handler) on or off.
  • Thank-you message — what the visitor sees after sending.
  • SMTP relay (host, port, authentication, username, password, STARTTLS, SSL/TLS, from address, "Send submissions to") — optional: with a blank host or recipient, submissions are only stored. The password is saved encrypted and never shown again. It belongs to the relay login it was typed for: a save that changes the host, port, username or a TLS switch without retyping the password clears it, and the settings page says so — enter it again. Send a test email checks the relay.

Anti-spam & privacy notes

The form is sent as a POST, so a visitor's name, email and message travel in the request body and never appear in a web address, their browser history or a proxy log. The platform lets this one form address accept a post from a visitor without a session (it takes nothing else and acts for nobody), and the form keeps its guards: a hidden honeypot field (a bot that fills it is silently ignored), your site's CAPTCHA when you configured one, and the platform's per-address rate limit.
