# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [2.1.0] — 2026-10-21

First public release of `blog`: blog entries as first-class virtual pages through the kernel's `ContentSource`
seam — each with its own URL (under a configurable prefix, or a custom path), rendered in the site's own chrome, in
every language the site serves, and listed in the sitemap and `llms.txt`. Requires SPI `1.0.0` (Runway `1.0.0` or later). Licensed under the GNU AGPL v3.0 with an additional permission for
plugins (`LICENSE`, `LICENSE-EXCEPTION`).
